# ChatGPT Business Plan Maker

A ChatGPT Skill that interviews you about your business and generates a
complete, professional business plan as a Microsoft Word document.

Turn a business idea into a structured business plan (.docx) through a
guided interview with ChatGPT. No blank page and no need to work through
a generic business-plan form yourself.

## What it does

-   Conducts a structured founder interview covering the information
    required to build the business plan
-   Asks questions in manageable groups rather than presenting one long
    form
-   Builds the plan section by section using the included business-plan
    template
-   Covers key areas including the executive summary, company
    description, market analysis, organization & management,
    products/services, marketing & sales strategy, operational plan,
    financial plan, and appendices
-   Adapts relevant sections depending on the company's ownership,
    funding strategy, and business model
-   Does not invent precise financial figures. Where confirmed figures
    are unavailable, projections and assumptions are clearly identified
-   Uses the information gathered during the interview to create a
    coherent, consistent plan
-   Writes the executive summary after the main plan has been developed
    so it reflects the completed business plan
-   Delivers the finished business plan as an editable Microsoft Word
    (.docx) document

## Requirements

-   Access to ChatGPT Skills on an eligible ChatGPT account or workspace
-   Permission to create or upload Skills in your workspace

Skill availability and permissions depend on your ChatGPT plan,
workspace, administrator settings, and current product availability.

## Installation

1.  Download `business-plan-maker.zip` from this repository or from the
    latest GitHub Release
2.  Open ChatGPT
3.  Select **Plugins** in the ChatGPT sidebar
4.  Open the **Skills** tab
5.  Select **Create**
6.  Select **Upload from your computer**
7.  Upload `business-plan-maker.zip`
8.  Allow ChatGPT to complete its Skill security scan
9.  Once installed, start a new ChatGPT conversation and ask for help
    creating a business plan

The ZIP contains the Skill instructions and supporting business-plan
template required by Business Plan Maker.

## Usage

Start a new ChatGPT conversation and describe your business idea or
existing company.

For example:

> "I'm starting a mobile dog grooming business. Can you help me create a
> business plan?"

ChatGPT can recognize when the Business Plan Maker Skill is relevant and
use it to guide the planning process.

It will interview you about the business, identify information still
required for the plan, develop the relevant sections, and create the
finished Word document.

You can also explicitly ask ChatGPT to use the Skill:

> "Use the Business Plan Maker Skill to create a business plan for my
> company."

## What's inside the Skill

The Business Plan Maker package contains:

-   `SKILL.md` --- the instructions ChatGPT follows to conduct the
    interview and build the business plan
-   `reference_template.docx` --- the business-plan template that
    defines the structure of the finished document
-   Supporting resources required by the Skill

The Skill is distributed as a ZIP package containing a single top-level
Skill folder.

## Business Plan Maker V1.0

This release is the first MVP version of Business Plan Maker.

Its purpose is deliberately focused:

**Interview → Structure → Plan → Word Document**

Business Plan Maker V1.0 focuses on gathering the information required
by the business-plan template, developing the plan, handling the
financial assumptions required by that plan, and producing the finished
document.

Detailed accounting models such as standalone 12-month profit-and-loss
statements, cash-flow forecasts, funding schedules, and advanced
financial modelling are not currently part of the V1.0 workflow.

These are being considered for future versions.
